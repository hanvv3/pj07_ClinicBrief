# ChatGPT source check

Checked on 2026-11-03 (Asia/Seoul) against `chatgpt-original.md` and the frozen protocol. This is an identified audit of the actual ChatGPT website response, not a blinded comparison or a verdict about which service is better. Public primary-source bodies were inspected; search excerpts alone were not counted as body verification. No patient records, reviews, private account data, or paid API calls were used.

## Findings

### 1. 실손24: electronic transmission versus additional certificates

- **Verified:** The [Ilsan Paik Hospital notice](https://www.paik.ac.kr/ilsan/user/bbs/BMSR00040/view.do?boardId=23887&menuNo=900104) is dated **2024-10-25**. Its body limits transmission to receipts, itemized statements and prescriptions, and expressly says additional documents such as a diagnosis certificate may be needed for inpatient claims or outpatient claims without a prescription. The old pharmacy implementation wording is present. The response correctly labels this as an old institutional notice, not a newly introduced rule or proof of widespread patient complaints.
- **Verified:** The [FSC notice](https://fsc.go.kr/po010103/87818) is dated **2026-09-30**. Section III states the three transmitted document types and the **2025-10-25** expansion to clinics/pharmacies. Its actual connection statistics distinguish statutory rollout from individual institutions being connected. This supports checking the clinic's connection status instead of promising availability.
- **Access limitation:** The cited [Silson24 customer-support page](https://www.silson24.or.kr/claim/web/custSupp/custSuppService) returned no readable body in the web reader; a direct HTTP fetch returned an application shell. Search indexing displayed the number, but that was not treated as direct body verification. **1811-3000** is independently present in the Paik notice and the opened [official Silson24 information PDF](https://www.silson24.or.kr/claim/dn/silson24_guide_document.pdf), which is an older 2024 implementation leaflet. Thus the number is corroborated, but its current support-page display and lack of a date were not independently reproduced.
- **Asset assessment:** No invented clinic fee, document-issuance rule, guaranteed claim payment, or assumed connection was found. Checking the exact insurer-requested document and the clinic's issuance procedure is an explicitly conditional local workflow. The claim-transmission/payment distinction is appropriate; the asset makes no coverage determination. Usable as the stated conditional response draft, with the listed local checks and current phone-number confirmation.

The body introduces lifecycle/context-specific privacy considerations and expressly includes use of commercial LLM services such as ChatGPT. The attached full guide was not independently audited; no claim here depends on its detailed legal provisions.
- **Asset assessment:** The source-grounded software claims are accurate. The prohibitions on patient/personnel uploads, director-designated approval, limited editing rights and review on job changes are **proposed clinic policy**, not OpenAI requirements or quoted Korean legal mandates. The response explicitly makes that distinction and limits adoption to a clinic that chooses a non-personal administrative/training use case. No unsupported privacy guarantee or claim that training opt-out authorizes patient-data use was found. Usable for that declared purpose after the stated administrator/local-policy checks.

### 3. Vaccine temperature excursions

- **Verified:** The [Yeongjong public-health notice](https://www.yeongjong.go.kr/health/pst/view.do?pst_id=hlth_ntc&pst_sn=333801) is registered **2026-09-21** and asks contracted vaccination institutions to monitor temperatures and inspect refrigerators/thermometers over Chuseok. It lists the 2025 storage guideline as an attachment. It is a preventive instruction, not evidence of a specific clinic accident or its prevalence, as the response correctly explains.
- **Verified by body and visual page inspection:** The linked [2025 KDCA storage guideline](https://www.kdca.go.kr/bbs/kdca/55/260179/download.do) has **2025.10** on its cover. Printed **pp. 31–33** (PDF pages **37–39**) support immediate reporting, use prohibition, segregation, documenting temperature/time/inventory, manufacturer assessment before disposal or reuse, and holiday/unknown-duration handling. Printed p. 32 expressly separates pre-supplied vaccines (report to the public-health center, then seek manufacturer quality assessment) from privately purchased vaccines (manufacturer assessment). The introductory notice says the guide is not legally binding and vaccine-specific guidance takes priority.
- **Citation defect, with independent corroboration:** The response's [2026 PDF link ending in `310053`](https://www.kdca.go.kr/bbs/kdca/55/310053/download.do) returned no readable content; a direct download produced **zero bytes**. Its exact version/content could not be verified. The [current official KDCA listing](https://www.kdca.go.kr/kdca/2861/subview.do?enc=Zm5jdDF8QEB8JTJGYmJzJTJGa2RjYSUyRjU1JTJGMzA5NjgzJTJGYXJ0Y2xWaWV3LmRvJTNG) records **2026-01-09** publication and **2026-10-02** modification, and links the [current medical-institution PDF ending in `310147`](https://www.kdca.go.kr/bbs/kdca/55/310147/download.do). That PDF was downloaded, text-extracted and visually inspected at **p. 79**: it confirms use-prohibition labeling, separate storage and manufacturer assessment before handling, and refers readers to the 2025 guide. This corroborates the advice but does not retroactively verify the broken original citation. The original's inability to find a PDF publication date is not a false date; the listing supplies useful omitted version metadata.
- **Asset assessment:** No critical unsafe advice was found within the stated unopened/refrigerated-vaccine scope and product-specific override. Normal refrigerator temperature is not treated as proof of vaccine usability; the draft appropriately holds use and disposal pending assessment. Recording an unknown time as unknown and involving the director are sensible proposed local procedures, not verbatim statutory requirements. The draft still needs the clinic's responsible staff, contacts and alternative storage plan, as it says. It is not a product-specific viability decision or a complete transport protocol. Update the 2026 citation before republication.
